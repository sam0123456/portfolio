ShiftPe — Gig Marketplace Platform

Location-aware platform connecting local businesses with college students for short-term gigs

MERN Stack

ShiftPe solves the discovery gap between local businesses needing short-term help and students looking for flexible earnings. Built with location-based proximity matching, mobile-first swipe navigation, and real-time chat.

  • Location-Based Search: Uses MongoDB Geospatial queries ($near) to calculate distance and restrict gig discovery to a 10km radius around the student.
  • Swipe-Based UI: Designed an intuitive, card-swiping interface inspired by modern mobile apps to let students quickly evaluate and apply to gigs.
  • Real-Time Messaging: Implemented WebSocket-based instant messaging (Socket.io) enabling accepted students and business owners to coordinate details immediately.
  • Dual-Role Authentication: Secure JWT authentication with partitioned schemas and permissions for business accounts vs. student profiles.

Hostel / PG Complaint Management System

Digital issue tracking & resolution system for hostel residents and administration

MERN Stack

Replaces manual paper registers in campus hostels with a transparent web application. Students can log maintenance tickets with status tracking, while wardens have a centralized dashboard to prioritize and resolve issues.

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Role-restricted routes ensuring students only view their submitted complaints, while wardens access all hostel rooms and triage tools.
  • Lifecycle State Tracking: Built a clear state flow (Pending → In Progress → Resolved) providing accountability for repairs and grievances.
  • Soft-Delete Architecture: Safely hides resolved complaints while retaining historical data in MongoDB for campus audit logs.
  • Management Dashboard: Wardens can filter by room, date, or category (electrical, plumbing, wifi) to quickly assign work.
React.js Node.js Express.js MongoDB RBAC REST API
